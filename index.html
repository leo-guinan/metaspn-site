The Philosophy

Competition raises consciousness.

When founders compete transparently, everyone learns faster. The game reveals patterns that solo building misses.

Small > Scale.

Better to master a small system than to fail at scale. Pods of 3-5 founders outperform solo efforts and massive accelerators.

Visibility = Trust.

Transparent metrics build trust faster than marketing. When progress is visible, investors and customers follow.

Shared risk → shared wisdom.

When founders share equity and metrics, they share knowledge. Collective intelligence emerges from aligned incentives.
