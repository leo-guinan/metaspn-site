01 · protocol

Not an affiliate program. A help graph.

A referral is not just attribution. It is a time edge: the person behind you in the network becomes the first human node your future-context requests can reach.

Connection

Referral receipts

Payment for creating the trusted edge. A connected user joins through a person, not a faceless funnel.

Search

Proof of search

When the first person cannot help, the request moves through the graph. Each route creates a receipt.

Energy Saved

Proof of energy saved

Queries that resolve from memory, corpus, cache, or human relay avoid external AI token spend. Those avoided exits create rebate receipts.

Line held: recruitment alone is not the product. The useful network is measured by how many hops it takes for missing context to reach the person who can resolve it.
02 · request path

Messages move backward through time.

STEP 01

A user hits the AI boundary.

The Guide can remember and compress, but it cannot pretend to know context outside its map.

STEP 02

The request goes to the person behind them.

The referrer receives the first relay because they created the connection.

STEP 03

The request can escalate.

If the first node cannot help, they route it up-chain or sideways to a better human node.

STEP 04

The useful answer writes back.

Accepted context becomes a diary receipt. The graph learns who helped, who routed, and how far the signal traveled.

03 · ledgers

Two ledgers. No hiding place.

Connection receipts pay for the edge. Energy rebates pay for useful work later. Mixing those ledgers turns a network into a pyramid with better typography. We are avoiding that, despite the obvious temptation.

04 · economics

Usage is help-based.

The expensive event is externalization. MetaSPN records which useful queries were resolved by memory, corpus, cache, prior resolutions, or human relay before they became fresh outside AI calls.

The communication is not on the AI layer. It is in the user network layer.

AI compresses. Humans route. Receipts settle. The graph becomes valuable when it can find the person who knows, not when it can pretend the model already does.

05 · substrate

Operating on Quai Network.

MetaSPN is designed as a metanetwork protocol on top of a work-based network: proof of search with proof of energy saved. Quai provides the energy-native substrate; MetaSPN records the human search and token-conservation layer above it.

Proof of search

Every escalation preserves the path: who searched, who routed, and which edge reduced uncertainty.

Proof of energy saved

Internal answers create energy-saved receipts when they prevent or reduce external AI token spend.

Metanetwork protocol

Networks need networks: humans behind humans, ledgers behind promises, and receipts behind trust.
